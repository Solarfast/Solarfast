---
name: Home-value and energy-label outcome claims
description: Evidence limits for heat-pump results discussing resale value and energy-label changes
---

For homeowner-facing calculations, describe efficiency as a possible factor in market appeal, not an assured or house-specific euro increase. Do not turn research or market associations into a guaranteed causal estimate. Show only the energy label the user entered; do not predict a future letter. Explain that an official label requires an assessor's determination.

**Why:** Market-level studies and general installation data do not value an individual home or calculate its post-project official label; those depend on the property, the work completed, and an assessor.

**How to apply:** Keep these limits in result pages, PDFs, and sales copy. Cite sources as context, not as a personal valuation or guarantee.
