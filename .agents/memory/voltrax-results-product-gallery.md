# Rule

Keep selected product photos in a clean horizontal, responsive row and align their visible bottoms/heights consistently. Use a shared photo stage, preserve each product's aspect ratio with `object-fit: contain`, and avoid cropping or stretching photos to force a match.

**Why:** The user has repeatedly asked for product photos on combination results to sit neatly beside one another at the same height.

**How to apply:** When changing result product cards, keep the cards side-by-side where the viewport allows, use equal photo-stage dimensions, and bottom-align contained images; verify at phone and desktop widths.
