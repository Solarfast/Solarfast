You are working on the VOLTRAX energy calculation engine.

This system is a commercial energy estimation engine for residential PV + battery + EMS systems in the Netherlands.

It must be strictly physically consistent. No assumptions outside defined formulas are allowed.

---

## ⚠️ HARD RULES (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

### 1. Energy conservation (absolute law)
PV energy is the ONLY source of energy.

Every kWh must be fully allocated:

PV_kWh =
    direct_self_consumption +
    battery_charge +
    grid_export

Load_kWh =
    direct_self_consumption +
    battery_discharge +
    grid_import

No energy creation, duplication, or loss outside battery efficiency.

---

### 2. No double counting
A single kWh cannot generate value in multiple places.

- PV value ≠ EMS value
- Battery shift ≠ extra generation
- EMS only values timing, not extra energy

---

### 3. EMS constraint (strict)
EMS operates ONLY on physically discharged battery energy.

ems_kwh ≤ battery_discharge_kwh

ems_kwh = min(
    battery_discharge_kwh,
    battery_charge_kwh * 1.2
)

ems_profit = ems_kwh × (grid_price - feed_in_price) × 0.35

No additional multipliers allowed.

---

### 4. Battery behavior (diminishing returns)
Battery effectiveness follows:

effectiveness = 1 - exp(-battery_kwh / 20)

This limits unrealistic scaling for large batteries.

---

## ⚙️ SELF-CONSUMPTION MODEL (STRICT FLOW DERIVED)

Self-consumption is NEVER an input or assumption.

It must be calculated from actual energy flows:

self_consumption = direct_self_consumption / PV_kWh

---

### WITHOUT BATTERY
Compute:

direct_self_consumption = min(PV_kWh, Load_kWh × 0.6)

This ensures realistic NL baseline:
- 30%–55% self consumption range typical

---

### WITH BATTERY

Step 1:
direct_self_consumption = min(PV_kWh, Load_kWh)

Step 2:
battery_charge = min(PV_kWh - direct_self_consumption, available_capacity_adjusted)

Step 3:
battery_discharge = battery_charge × efficiency

Step 4:
battery_used_for_load = min(battery_discharge, Load_kWh - direct_self_consumption)

---

## ⚡ GRID FLOWS

grid_export = PV_kWh - direct_self_consumption - battery_charge
grid_import = Load_kWh - direct_self_consumption - battery_used_for_load

---

## 📊 OUTPUT REQUIREMENTS

Return EXACTLY:

- self_consumption_before (%)
- self_consumption_after (%)
- grid_import_before / after (kWh)
- grid_export_before / after (kWh)
- battery_charge_kwh
- battery_discharge_kwh
- ems_kwh
- total_annual_value_eur

---

## 📉 REALISM CONSTRAINTS (HARD CAPS)

Reject or clamp outputs if:

- self_consumption_before not in 30%–55%
- self_consumption_after > 98%
- total annual value > €3,300 for residential system
- EMS contributes > 60% of total value
- grid_export_after = 0

---

## 🎯 GOAL

Produce a physically correct, non-exploitable, stable energy model where:

- Battery = energy shifting only
- EMS = timing optimization only
- Value = derived strictly from real kWh flows

No overlaps. No hidden multipliers. No synthetic gains.
